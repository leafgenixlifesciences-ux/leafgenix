## 6. Design system observations

- **Type:** headlines in a heavy grotesque (Arial Black / Helvetica Bold family); body in plain Arial. Product wordmarks are custom-drawn, each with its own colour identity — the house style deliberately lets each brand look different.
- **Layout:** A3 landscape, claim headline top-centre, evidence bullets left, product pack shot right, benefit strapline bottom-left, logo bottom-centre on a yellow halo.
- **Signature devices:** sky-to-lime gradient background · yellow halo behind the footer logo · colour-coded claim boxes · reference citations in tiny superscript.
- **Tone:** evidence-first and citation-heavy — journal references, clinical-trial data, GOR grading tables. That is a real asset for the website: this brand can credibly lead with published research.

## 7. What this means for leafgenix.in

The site currently in `Desktop\Coding\leafgenix` was built with **placeholder products that do not exist** (Immunocore, Curcumin Prime, Ashwa Calm, Livgen, Omega Pure, Fembalance). The real catalogue above should replace them.

Recommended for the e-commerce storefront — the 8 nutraceuticals, which need no prescription:

1. SYNVIT-Forte · 2. Probion · 3. EDO WELL · 4. L-SHARP 400 · 5. MD3 Nano Shot · 6. Perfect Liv · 7. Haemerange · 8. Calcin-K27

The 21 Rx products are best presented as a non-transactional "Our Range / For Healthcare Professionals" section — listing them for sale online would need a retail drug licence and prescription verification.

The site's current palette (bone/forest/lime) should be re-based on the real brand colours: **`#005C2D` green + `#ED940D` orange**, which are already close in spirit to what was built.
