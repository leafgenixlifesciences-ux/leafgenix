# SEO action plan

## Completed

- Technical crawl/indexing foundation: sitemap, robots, canonical URLs, server-rendered metadata and structured data.
- Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Search Console property setup.
- Live Google Business Profile with consistent public address, phone and hours.
- Product, merchant-listing and breadcrumb enhancements validated in Search Console.
- Five source-backed, crawlable journal articles.

## Next actions

1. **Google verification (high):** complete the Google Business Profile verification flow when available.
2. **Index monitoring (high):** review Search Console indexing after data processing completes; inspect only URLs that remain excluded or have errors.
3. **Reputation (high):** ask genuine customers for honest reviews without incentives or filtering.
4. **Business listings (medium):** create/claim Bing Places and Apple Business Connect entries using exactly the same name, address, phone and hours.
5. **Content (medium):** add one genuinely useful, medically cautious article each month and link it from relevant product/category pages.
6. **Experience (medium):** assess mobile Core Web Vitals once Search Console has enough field data.

## Do not do

- Do not buy backlinks, reviews or directory packages.
- Do not create thin Jaipur keyword pages or make unverified medical claims.
- Do not repeatedly resubmit sitemaps or request indexing for every page; Google schedules its own crawl.
